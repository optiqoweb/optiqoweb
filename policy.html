Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Optiqo collects when you get in touch with us or work with us on a project, and how that information is used.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you share with us — such as your name, email address, phone number, or WhatsApp number — when you message us through the site, email, phone, or WhatsApp.
  • Project details you provide, including business information, content, images, and preferences needed to design and build your website or graphics.
  • Messages you send through the on-site chat assistant, which are used only to answer your question during that session.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and discuss your project.
  • To design, build, and deliver the website, branding, or graphic design work you've asked for.
  • To send project updates, invoices, or follow-ups related to work you've asked us to do.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except where needed to deliver your project (for example, a hosting provider or a Google Sheet used as your site's backend, if that's part of what we're building for you).

Data storage & security

Depending on the project, your information may be stored in tools like Google Sheets or Google Apps Script, email, or WhatsApp — the same everyday tools most small businesses already use. We take reasonable steps to keep this information secure, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by reaching out through the Contact page.

Cookies & analytics

Our website itself does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics beyond what's needed for it to function. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

Terms of Service

These terms outline what to expect when you hire Optiqo for web design & development, graphic design & branding, or digital solutions work.

Our process

Projects generally follow three steps: we talk through what you need, we design and build with your sign-off along the way, and then we launch and support the finished product. Timelines vary by scope, but most small business projects take about 1–2 weeks once a design is approved.

Client responsibilities

  • Providing the content, images, and feedback we need in a timely manner, since delays on your end can extend the project timeline.
  • Reviewing draft work and giving clear, timely approval or revision requests at each stage.
  • Ensuring you have the rights to any text, images, or brand assets you provide us to use in your project.

Payment

Pricing depends on the scope of each project and is agreed before work begins. Unless otherwise arranged in writing, an upfront deposit is typically required to start work, with the remaining balance due before launch or upon delivery.

Revisions

A reasonable number of revisions is included as part of each project to make sure the result matches what was agreed. Requests that go significantly beyond the original scope may be treated as additional work and quoted separately.

Ownership

Once a project is paid for in full, you own the final deliverables (such as your website files or final graphic designs). Optiqo may showcase completed work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Third-party costs

Costs like domain names, hosting, or paid stock assets are not included in our design/development fees unless specifically stated, and are billed separately or paid directly by the client.

Limitation of liability

We aim to deliver reliable, well-built work, but Optiqo isn't liable for indirect losses arising from the use of a delivered website, design, or digital solution, including third-party service outages beyond our control.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

We want projects to work out well for everyone, so here's how cancellations and refunds are handled.

Before work begins

If you cancel before any design or development work has started, your deposit will be refunded in full, minus any payment processing fees already incurred.

After work has started

If you cancel after work is underway, the deposit (or a portion of it reflecting work already completed) is non-refundable, since it covers time and resources already spent on your project. Any additional milestone payments already made will be assessed based on work delivered up to that point.

After delivery or launch

Once a project has been delivered, approved, and launched, payments are final and non-refundable. If something isn't working as agreed, please reach out — we'll fix genuine issues covered by the original scope.

How to request a cancellation or refund

Message us on WhatsApp or email through the Contact page and we'll walk through the details of your specific project together.
